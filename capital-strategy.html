Overview

Two rounds. One capital plan.
Founder majority preserved through both.

Round 1 is an F&F refinance priced against the organic valuation. Round 2 is a proper seed priced against the Acquisition Growth Valuation, sized to fund two valet-operator acquisitions (Indianapolis + Denver). Both rounds sit inside a 1,000,000-share starting cap with standard Option-C issuance mechanics.

Why two rounds, not one: Round 1 gives F&F investors organic-priced entry with acquisition-case upside — a strong return for people who move on relationship. Round 2 investors get acquisition-priced entry with expansion upside — sized to what an institutional check will underwrite. Trying to raise a single blended round leaves both audiences unhappy. This structure lets each priced against the anchor that fits them.
What the advisor needs to weigh in on: Round 2 pre-money at $12M is a placeholder that produces a 2.6× base-case MOIC — thin for institutional seed pricing. Options include (a) lower R2 pre-money, (b) smaller R2 raise, (c) higher R2 ownership target, (d) lean harder on the acquisitions/expansion upside so R2 base case is $55M-exit not $36M.
