# Columbus, OH — Market Inventory Memo
**Sophi Mobility Expansion v1 | May 2026**

---

## Market Overview

Columbus is Ohio's capital (~920,000 population, metro ~2.1M). The CBD is compact: the Greater Columbus Convention Center, Nationwide Arena, Lower.com Field, and the hotel row on High Street / Nationwide Blvd are within a 10-minute walk. Secondary clusters include German Village / Brewery District (south), the OSU–Wexner Medical corridor (northwest), Easton Town Center (northeast), and Bridge Park / Dublin (suburbs).

Key demand drivers: Columbus Blue Jackets (NHL), Columbus Crew (MLS), Columbus Clippers (Triple-A), Ohio State University (Schottenstein Center), the GCCC (~1M annual attendees), Short North gallery/nightlife, and OSU Wexner Medical Center. The Hilton Columbus Downtown (1,000 rooms) is Ohio's largest hotel; its GCCC attachment makes it the market's highest-volume valet anchor.

---

## Operator Landscape

**SP+** dominates CBD parking structures including Nationwide Arena and GCCC garages. **Towne Park** is the probable operator at major flag hotels (Hilton, Hyatt Regency, Renaissance). **CASE (Columbus Arena Sports & Entertainment)** manages Nationwide Arena and Schottenstein Center event parking. **PromoWest / AEG** controls KEMBA Live and Express Live.

Independents include Lindey's (complimentary in-house), Shadowbox Live, and most Short North restaurants.

**Cameron Mitchell Restaurants** controls 5+ accounts (Mitchell's Steakhouse, Butcher & Rose, Marcella's, The Guild House, The Barn) — a strong group-multiplier target.

---

## Top 5 Highest-Priority Accounts

1. **Hilton Columbus Downtown** — 1,000 rooms, attached to GCCC, highest absolute valet volume in the market. Likely Towne Park; ripe for displacement or co-management.
2. **Hyatt Regency Columbus** — 633 rooms, GCCC-connected, valet confirmed at $35/day. Convention co-anchor with Hilton.
3. **Nationwide Arena** — 20,000-seat NHL home; 120 event days/year; proximity to Hotel row creates overflow parking demand.
4. **OSU Wexner Medical Center** — 1,154 beds, 1.86M outpatient visits/year; patient/visitor valet is a high-frequency revenue stream with clinical-grade service expectations.
5. **Renaissance Columbus Downtown** — 408 rooms, $45 valet confirmed, Marriott managed; historically Sophi's strongest conversion segment nationally.

---

## Notable Risks

- **Operator entrenchment:** SP+ has deep structural roots in Columbus CBD garages; displacing them from venue lot management will require hotel-side anchor wins first.
- **Ownership concentration:** Marriott International controls ~5 CBD properties (Renaissance, Sheraton, Westin, AC, Courtyard, Residence Inn, Moxy, Le Méridien). A single brand-level gate could block or unlock multiple accounts simultaneously.
- **Cameron Mitchell cluster:** 5 restaurant accounts are controlled by one operator. A group contract is the right approach but creates single-point-of-failure risk if rejected.
- **Seasonality:** OSU football (Ohio Stadium, not in this inventory) drives massive parking demand in fall that overshadows other venues; Schottenstein and Nationwide Arena have complementary NHL/concert peaks in winter/spring.
- **Kihachi (known anchor):** Confirmed permanently closed December 2018; excluded from inventory.
