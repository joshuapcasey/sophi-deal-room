# Fort Wayne, Indiana — Market Inventory Memo
**Market Key:** `ft_wayne` | **Accounts catalogued:** 65 | **Date:** May 2026

---

## Market Overview

Fort Wayne is Indiana's second-largest city (~270,000 metro). The CBD is compact, anchored by the **Grand Wayne Convention Center** (225,000 sf, 2nd largest in Indiana) flanked by three attached hotels — Hilton (246 rooms), Courtyard (250 rooms), and Hampton Inn (136 rooms). The **downtown event district** runs along Jefferson and Superior Streets with Parkview Field (8,100+ cap), Embassy Theatre (2,471 seats), and a dense restaurant cluster along The Landing and Harrison Street. The **southwest corridor** (W. Jefferson Blvd/I-69 Exit 302) hosts Lutheran Hospital (396 beds) plus four select-service hotels. The **north corridor** contains the Allen County War Memorial Coliseum (13,000 cap), Hilton Garden Inn North, TownePlace Suites, and Hyatt Place Northwest. Demand drivers are convention/event (GWCC + Coliseum), healthcare (Parkview Health + Lutheran Health Network), minor-league sports (TinCaps, Komets), and a maturing downtown F&B scene.

---

## Operator Landscape

No confirmed national valet operator presence was identified. The Bradley Hotel includes valet in the room rate (Provenance in-house). Hilton and Courtyard rely on self-park garages at $15/day. Parkview Regional has temporarily suspended valet. The Coliseum self-operates 5,500 surface spaces at $8–$12/event. The restaurant scene — Club Soda, Gas House, Proximo, Copper Spoon — relies on adjacent public garages with no contracted valet operators identified. **The market appears largely greenfield for a national valet operator.**

## Top 5 Priority Accounts

1. **Hilton Fort Wayne at the Grand Wayne Convention Center** — 246-room convention hotel, direct GWCC connection, self-park garage at $15/day; highest event volume in market.
2. **Grand Wayne Convention Center** — 225,000 sf, 4,500-seat theater, 3,080 banquet; 150+ event-days; attached garages prime for managed parking.
3. **Courtyard by Marriott Fort Wayne Downtown** — 250 rooms, GWCC co-anchor, $15/day self-park, high convention overflow demand.
4. **Parkview Regional Medical Center** — 823 beds, valet temporarily suspended (re-entry opportunity), dedicated parking garage on north campus.
5. **Allen County War Memorial Coliseum** — 13,000-cap arena + 108,000 sf expo, 1M+ annual visitors; premium/VIP parking gap in self-operated surface lots.

## Notable Risks

- **Operator gates:** The Bradley's valet is captive to Provenance; Hilton/Marriott properties require brand-approved parking vendors — expect RFP hurdles.
- **Ownership concentration:** Don Hall's controls 6–7 locations; one relationship unlocks a family of accounts, but one refusal blocks all of them.
- **Low valet culture:** Driving-heavy Midwest market; restaurant valet_conv defaults may overstate demand — field validation recommended.
- **Seasonality:** Foellinger Theatre is summer-only; TinCaps season (April–September) inflates downtown parking materially; GWCC peaks spring/fall.
- **Hospital valet pause:** Parkview Regional valet temporarily suspended — confirm reinstatement before modeling healthcare revenue.
