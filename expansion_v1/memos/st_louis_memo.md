# St. Louis MO — Market Inventory Memo
**Market Key:** st_louis | **Accounts Catalogued:** 79 | **Date:** May 2026

---

## Market Overview

St. Louis presents a compact, walkable downtown core anchored by three distinct demand zones:

1. **Downtown / Ballpark Village / Arch District** — The highest-density valet zone, centered on Busch Stadium and Enterprise Center. Hotels here include 910-room Hyatt Regency, 917-room Marriott Grand, 671-room Hilton at the Ballpark, and the 567-room Union Station Curio. Event overlay is extreme: MLB (81 home Cardinals games), NHL Blues (~60 home games + playoffs), and the Stifel Theatre / America's Center / Dome corridor generate year-round evening demand.

2. **Clayton CBD** — St. Louis County's financial and legal hub with a walkable restaurant district. The Ritz-Carlton (299 rooms), Le Méridien (268 rooms), Sheraton Clayton Plaza (~258 rooms), and the new AC Hotel (207 rooms) serve a high-income corporate clientele. Tony's, Capital Grille, two Ruth's Chris locations, Pastaria, and Herbie's concentrate fine-dining valet demand within a 6-block radius.

3. **Central West End / Forest Park / Cortex** — Medical, cultural, and lifestyle corridor. Chase Park Plaza (398 rooms) anchors the CWE hotel market. Barnes-Jewish Hospital (1,266 beds) and the broader BJC/WashU campus drive year-round hospital valet demand. The Fox Theatre, Powell Hall, and Jazz Bistro create evening entertainment clustering in Grand Center.

Additional zones include Soulard (Sidney Street Cafe, Blood & Sand, Vin de Set), Maplewood (Acero), Webster Groves (Olive + Oak, Robust), and West County (Annie Gunn's, J. Gilbert's).

## Operator Landscape

**National operators:**
- **SP+** dominates the downtown parking garage infrastructure — confirmed at Hyatt Regency (Arch garages), Marriott Grand (parking), Enterprise Center, Busch Stadium, and America's Center.
- **Towne Park** appears at Hilton St. Louis at the Ballpark, Four Seasons, Chase Park Plaza Royal Sonesta, The Ritz-Carlton, and St. Louis Union Station Hotel — the premium hotel tier.
- **LAZ Parking** operates at Hilton Pennywell and Embassy Suites, and has confirmed garages in the CWE/Clayton corridors.

**Independents:** A large share of Clayton, CWE boutique, and suburban restaurant accounts operate with local or in-house valet. Notable white-space accounts include Angad Arts Hotel, Last Hotel, Magnolia, and the majority of chef-driven restaurants.

## Notable Risks

- **Operator Gates:** Towne Park holds the premium hotel tier (Hilton Ballpark, Four Seasons, Ritz-Carlton, Union Station). SP+ controls the downtown garage infrastructure. Displacing either requires long contract cycles.
- **Ownership Concentration:** Marriott manages or franchises 10+ properties (Marriott Grand, Westin, Magnolia/Tribute, Le Méridien, Sheraton Clayton, Ritz-Carlton, AC, Courtyard, Residence Inn, Aloft). A single Marriott brand relationship affects the largest portion of the hotel TAM.
- **Seasonality:** The Muny (70 summer nights) and Cardinals season (April–October) create extreme spring/summer peaks. Winter demand softens for event venues. Peak_mod 1.10–1.20 applied to stadium and Ballpark Village accounts.
- **Clayton vs. Downtown Split:** Clayton operates as a semi-independent submarket. Decision-makers for Ritz-Carlton, Le Méridien, Tony's, and Capital Grille are distinct from downtown.
