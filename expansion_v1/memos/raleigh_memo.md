## Market Overview

The Raleigh–Durham–Cary triangle is one of the fastest-growing US metros, anchored by three distinct cores: Downtown Raleigh (Fayetteville Street / Glenwood South / Warehouse District), Downtown Durham (American Tobacco District), and North Hills. RTP and the RDU corridor layer dense corporate travel demand year-round.

The Raleigh Convention Center (500,000 sqft; $425M expansion underway) drives overflow into six adjacent full-service hotels. Glenwood South is the market's dining and nightlife spine with strong weekend valet demand. Durham's American Tobacco cluster — DPAC, Durham Bulls Athletic Park, 21c Museum Hotel, and Washington Duke Inn — forms a concentrated sub-market comparable to Charlotte's South End.

## Operator Landscape

- **LAZ Parking** — probable primary operator at Lenovo Center; confirmed valet presence at the new Homewood Suites/Tempo by Hilton Raleigh Downtown (shared address).
- **SP+** — most likely operator for the convention center, stadium corridors, and amphitheatre surface lots.
- **Marriott managed** — controls valet at 11 of 30 hotels in inventory (Sheraton, City Center Marriott, Residence Inn, Renaissance, AC Hotel, StateView, Aloft, Marriott Crabtree, Marriott RTP, Westin RDU, Courtyard Midtown).
- **Hilton managed** — controls valet at 9 hotels (Tempo, Homewood Suites, Hilton North Hills, Embassy Suites ×2, DoubleTree ×2, Hyatt House, Hyatt Place).
- **Duke University** — captive ownership of Washington Duke Inn and JB Duke Hotel; decisions likely centralized.
- **Independents:** The Umstead Hotel (SAS-affiliated), Origin Hotel, The Longleaf, The Durham Hotel, and Angus Barn operate proprietary or locally contracted parking.

## Top 5 Anchor Candidates

1. **Raleigh Marriott City Center** — 401 rooms connected to the convention center; highest downtown foot-traffic hotel; strong valet capture opportunity.
2. **The Umstead Hotel and Spa** — luxury/resort, 150 rooms near RDU; occ ~72%, valet_conv 0.55; confirmed valet culture.
3. **Washington Duke Inn & Golf Club** — 271 rooms, Duke-owned resort/conference; peak_mod 1.10 for event/graduation seasons.
4. **Renaissance Raleigh North Hills** — 229-room lifestyle hotel; North Hills cluster (AC Hotel, Hyatt House) enables multi-property approach.
5. **Martin Marietta Center for the Performing Arts** — 4-hall complex (2,354-seat main hall); 200+ event days/year; downtown adjacency creates integrated demand model.

## Notable Risks

- **Operator gatekeeping:** LAZ and SP+ are embedded in arena/stadium/convention parking. Displacement requires demonstrating tech-enabled yield improvement to venue operators.
- **Brand-managed concentration:** 20 of 30 hotels are Marriott- or Hilton-managed; negotiations may require brand or management-company approvals rather than individual GMs. Washington Duke Inn and JB Duke Hotel share Duke University ownership — decisions are likely centralized.
- **Seasonality:** Outdoor amphitheatres (Red Hat, Walnut Creek, Booth, NC Museum of Art) are spring–fall only. Stadium events peak in fall football season.
- **Garland closure noted:** The known anchor "Garland" closed its Raleigh location in August 2022. Chef Cheetie Kumar's follow-on restaurant **Ajja** (Five Points, 2023) is included in inventory as the active replacement.
