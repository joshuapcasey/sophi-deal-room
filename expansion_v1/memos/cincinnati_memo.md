# Cincinnati, OH — Market Inventory Memo
**Sophi Mobility Expansion v1 | May 2026**

---

## Market Overview

Cincinnati's valet-relevant geography spans three dense clusters. **The Banks** is the highest-priority zone: Great American Ball Park, Paycor Stadium, Heritage Bank Center, Duke Energy Convention Center (reopened Jan 2026, $264M renovation), and Andrew J. Brady Music Center form a continuous event corridor within 0.3 miles of the AC Hotel, Moxy, and multiple Marriott brands. **Downtown CBD** (Fountain Square to OTR) holds the flagship convention hotels — Hilton Netherland Plaza, Hyatt Regency, Westin, Renaissance — plus the densest upscale restaurant cluster in the region (Boca, Sotto, Orchids, Prime, Jeff Ruby's, The Davidson). **Uptown/Clifton** is anchored by UC Medical Center and Cincinnati Children's Hospital, a healthcare corridor with constant high-volume parking demand, served by the new Hotel Celare. The Covington/Newport riverfront adds 6 hotels and Lisse Steakhuis, connected to downtown by the Roebling Bridge walkway.

---

## Operator Landscape

**SP+** is the dominant national operator with 15+ downtown garages and stadium/convention-center contracts. **LAZ Parking** holds the 3rd & Main garage and covers the Hilton Netherland Plaza block. **ASM Global** manages both Duke Energy Convention Center and Heritage Bank Center. No confirmed Towne Park or Propark presence exists in the downtown core; most upscale hotel valet runs via SP+ or in-house. Jeff Ruby's and Prime Cincinnati use Star Parking and independent operators respectively.

---

## Top 5 Anchor Candidates

1. **Hilton Cincinnati Netherland Plaza** — 561 rooms, Art Deco landmark, AAA Four Diamond, valet at $45; GF Hotels managing; high group/convention mix; strongest single-hotel revenue profile.
2. **Hyatt Regency Cincinnati** — 491 rooms, 40,000 sq ft meeting space, Davidson/Pivot managed; directly adjacent to convention center; valet/parking a daily operational requirement.
3. **Renaissance Cincinnati Downtown Hotel** — 323 rooms, $49 valet confirmed, Marriott managed; best-performing Marriott brand in the CBD event zone.
4. **Great American Ball Park / The Banks corridor** — 81 home games + concerts; ac Hotel and Moxy adjacent; a multi-property zone contract covering banks-area hotel + stadium parking is the highest-upside single pitch.
5. **UC Medical Center + Cincinnati Children's Hospital** — combined 1,282 licensed beds, daily visitor and staff parking volume; single-campus dual-hospital pitch opportunity.

---

## Notable Risks

- **Operator gates**: SP+ has deep entrenched relationships at both stadiums and the convention center; displacing them requires a strong Sophi operational pitch on revenue uplift.
- **Marriott concentration**: 11 of 25 hotels are Marriott-branded; ownership is diffuse (Host Hotels REIT, independent franchisees, Marriott-managed). Individual GMs have limited contract authority.
- **Seasonality**: Riverbend Music Center and Paycor Stadium are highly seasonal (summer concerts, 10-game NFL season); banks-area hotels show peak_mod 1.10–1.30 during events.
- **Cross-river split**: 6 key properties are in Covington/Newport, KY — separate municipality requiring Kentucky operator licensing.
