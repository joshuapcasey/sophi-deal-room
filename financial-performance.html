2026 anchor

$1.38M gross · projected from booked

$593,740 booked Jan–Aug against the 62.3% seasonal earnings share extrapolates to $953K. The remaining $427K comes from the four late-2026 Charlotte anchors (Savu, Cascade, G&G, Fogo) plus Q4 seasonal lift.

Y3 step-up

$2.10M → $3.70M in 2029

The 76% step is where Denver, Indianapolis, and Louisville each cross the operator-credibility gate that unlocks the LAZ and Towne-Park hotel books. Charlotte holds; the step is expansion revenue.

Y5 ceiling

25% EBIT · sustained, not peaked

Margin ceiling is the SOPHI operating record, not an aggressive projection. The JSK turnaround (28% → 72% net) shows the top of the range. 25% EBIT at 15-market scale is the middle of the observed operating band.

Not in this P&L

Blend Wealth is the organic plan. Acquisitions of adjacent operator portfolios, hospitals as a full vertical, airport concessions, SpotHero online-valet arbitrage, SOPHI ad-sales and SMS revenue, cross-property referral fees, and franchise royalties are all outside the model — carried instead against the valuation, where they're priced as upside optionality.
