Assumptions · locked to the deal room model
Revenue
  • Y1 2027 · $1.72M — Organic Growth Model revenue: Charlotte carry plus initial Denver and Indianapolis wins. No SMS ad layer, no acquisitions.
  • Y2 2028 · $2.10M — organic ramp continues as Denver and Indianapolis compound; still no ancillary revenue streams.
  • Monthly weighting follows the Charlotte 62.3% seasonal earnings curve (H2-weighted).
Margin
  • Gross margin 50.0% → 51.0% over 24 months, consistent with the Organic Growth Model P&L.
  • Direct labor + ops variable with revenue; no negative operating leverage above baseline.
  • Ad-layer marginal margin ~90% (SMS delivery cost only), included in blended GM.
Opex
  • G&A $40K/mo Y1 → $55K/mo Y2 (founder draw, admin, insurance base).
  • Product/eng $12K → $25K/mo (Bryce ramp + Hub build → support).
  • Sales $8K → $20K/mo (licensing motion ramps post-Hub MVP).
  • Market launch — one-time: Denver $75K (Q1/Q2), Indy $45K (Q3/Q4).
Cash floor
  • Working capital reserve ($40K of the raise) held as a floor buffer, released only if revenue lands ahead of forecast.
  • Cash never dips below the 9-month forward runway threshold under this schedule.
  • If Charlotte revenue underdelivers by 20%, min cash still holds >$300K.
Below the line
  • Cash taxes · 15% of positive operating cash flow (partial provision, LLC pass-through).
  • Interest · $1K/mo on existing line, no new debt in this window.
  • D&A · excluded from cash view (non-cash); tracked separately on the P&L.
What could break this
  • Charlotte Q1 2027 revenue < $150K (seasonal risk if late-2026 anchors slip).
  • Denver launch delayed past May 2027 (currently gated on G&G contract execution).
  • Hub MVP slips past July 2027, forcing continued split of Bryce's time — see Risk #3.
Path to Series A

At end of Y2 (Dec 2028), SOPHI is at $2M+ organic revenue run-rate, cash-generative, with $5.19M on the balance sheet. That's the position from which the Series A conversation happens — sized to fund Expansion Markets and the hospital vertical. Expected raise: $8–15M at $20–35M pre. Dilution modeled at 15–20%. This round does not need to bridge to a Series A of desperation; the operating cash flow does that on its own.
