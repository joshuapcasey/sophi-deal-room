The four rules that produce the curve
01

Binary acquisition

Each in-SAM account has a single acquisition year (Y1–Y5) or stays unacquired. Before that year, $0. From that year forward, full annual TAM. No fractional ramp — a steakhouse generates the same revenue in month 13 as in month 60.

02

Operator credibility gates

SOPHI can't displace national operators (Towne Park, LAZ, SP+, ABM, Ace, Reef, Metropolis) without a floor: min accounts already won × min verticals proven. A Towne-operated hotel doesn't unlock until SOPHI has 8 wins across 3 verticals. In-house, independent, and SOPHI-already accounts have no gate.

03

Relationship multiplier

Sister-property wins compound — Darden, Hilton-family, Marriott-family, Omni. Once SOPHI lands one property in an ownership group: 1 prior win → 1.5×, 2 → 2×, 3+ → 3× WAS lift. Charlotte's 4 Darden anchors seed the multiplier on every Darden property in the six-market portfolio.

04

Conservative per-market caps

Y5 penetration ceiling: 50% of SAM for Charlotte and Indianapolis (operational data supports it), 30% for Phoenix, Denver, Cleveland, Louisville. S-curve trajectory (10/30/50/70/85) scales each year to the cap. Material upside sits above the cap: occupancy lift, valet conversion, brand-flag network effects, post-Y5 penetration.

Not in the curve — deliberate omissions

The v3 engine models parking-services revenue only. It does not model hospitals as a full vertical, airports, SpotHero online-valet arbitrage, SOPHI ad-sales and SMS revenue, cross-property referral services, or franchise royalties. These are catalogued in the valuation callout and priced against the deal, not against the operating plan.
