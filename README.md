## The raise

**$500K seed** at **$4.5M pre / $5.0M post** — 10% seed equity at $45.00/share. Priced round OR post-money SAFE @ $5M cap. $25K minimum check. Runway through end of Y2 (Dec 2028) with Series A raised from a $5.19M cash position.

## Returns

Same $500K seed at 10% · 5-year hold from Jan 2027 close to end of 2031.

| Scenario | Exit equity | Seed value | MOIC | IRR |

| Downside — miss Y5 by one year | $3.89M | $389K | 0.78× | −4.8% |
| SP+ trading comp @ 0.7× revenue | $19.80M | $1.98M | 3.96× | 31.7% |
| **v3 DCF anchor — parking only (base)** | **$36.30M** | **$3.63M** | **7.26×** | **48.7%** |
| **v3 DCF anchor — parking + SMS ad** | **$47.36M** | **$4.74M** | **9.47×** | **56.8%** |

## What's in the deal room

15 screens across 5 groups:

- **Overview** — Summary (with The Ask), Company History
- **The opportunity** — SOPHI OS (Hub · Staffing · Revenue), Growth Model (six-market map, drilldown)
- **The deal** — Use of Funds, Cash Model, Financial Performance, Organic Valuation, Growth Valuation, Competition, Risk Mitigating Milestones
- **Diligence** — Methodology, Documents (14 artifacts), Questions
- **Firm** — Your Access

Plus the 12-slide pitch deck PDF as an instant-download in the Documents tab.
