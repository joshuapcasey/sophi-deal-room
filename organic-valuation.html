Revenue streams not in this valuation

The DCF above is priced on parking services revenue only. Hospitals as a full vertical, airport concessions, SpotHero online-valet arbitrage, SOPHI ad-sales and SMS revenue, cross-property referral services, and franchise royalties are all excluded. Every stream is real, catalogued, and available on request — they are held out of the model to make the base case defensible, not to hide upside.
