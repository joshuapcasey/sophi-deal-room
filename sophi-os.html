The opportunity

SOPHI OS.
The operating layer, the people layer, and the money layer.

SOPHI operates as one system across every property. The hub is the software, the staffing model is how the shifts get filled, and the revenue model is how each property is priced. Three views of the same operating unit.

Operations

Incidents & write-ups

Every service-recovery event, damage claim, or friction point captured in the same schema across every property.

  • Photo, timestamp, GPS on every entry
  • Auto-routing by severity and vertical
  • Insurance-grade paper trail
Operations

Shift daily reports

The single artifact GMs open every morning: last night's covers, revenue, tips, incidents, and staffing gaps, per property, in one view.

  • Auto-generated at 03:00 local time
  • Owner-configurable KPI set
  • Text-first, mobile-first
Growth

Sales

The account book: which prospect, in which vertical, under which operator, at what credibility gate — the pipeline the six-market model runs on.

  • 203 accounts under active tracking
  • Operator-gate awareness built in
  • Relationship-multiplier tagging
Growth

Marketing & reviews

Every guest interaction routed to a review at the right moment. Property-level review sentiment fed back into the shift report.

  • 3× to 5× review-volume lift
  • Negative-review interception path
  • Owner-visible sentiment index
Insight

Reporting

Owner dashboards fed by the same schema powering operations. No manual roll-up. No spreadsheet drift.

  • Live per-property P&L
  • Portfolio and multi-brand roll-ups
  • Export-ready for lender / investor
People

Academy

Onboarding, upskilling, and cross-property mobility. The mechanism behind <5% churn and 82% no-wait service in a category that averages 1%.

  • Role-based curriculum
  • Property-transfer certification
  • GM promotion path
Architecture posture

Config-driven accounts

Every property is a config object. New verticals ship as configuration, not code.

Zero-trust by role and tag

Reads gated by property, role, and tag. Cross-tenant access requires explicit provisioning.

Kill-switched outbound

Every outbound integration (SMS, review platform, POS) behind a per-property switch.

Vendor-neutral AI

Model layer swappable. No dependency on a single model provider for the operating loop.
