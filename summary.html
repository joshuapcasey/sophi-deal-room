Overview

Two rounds. One capital plan.
Advisor working file for CFO review.

SOPHI is raising a $500K F&F refinance (Round 1) priced against the Organic Growth Valuation, then a $4.5M Acquisition Seed (Round 2) priced against the Acquisition Growth Valuation to fund two valet-operator acquisitions (Indianapolis + Denver). This deal room is a working file to help a new CFO and business advisor finalize the actual terms, valuation, and deal structure before the F&F raise goes live.

Working file · What this deck is for

This is a working file for finalizing SOPHI's two-round capital strategy with a CFO and business advisor — not a live investor deal room.

Purpose: The current numbers, share counts, valuations, and use-of-funds allocations are Joshua's working thesis for how the F&F raise (Round 1) and the acquisition seed (Round 2) should be structured. The advisor's job is to pressure-test and finalize.

After advisor sign-off: This working file gets tailored down into a Round-2-only investor room for the acquisition seed raise. Round 1 F&F closes first, based on the terms finalized here.

Start here: Capital Strategy shows the two-round plan. Advisor Questions lists the ten open decisions the CFO/advisor needs to weigh in on.
